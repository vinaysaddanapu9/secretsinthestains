📘 Who We Are

Bridging Academic Knowledge with Real-World Forensics

Secrets in the Stains is a forensic science education and training platform designed to help students move beyond theoretical learning and gain practical, career-oriented exposure to forensic investigation concepts.

Our mission is to create accessible, structured, and professionally designed learning experiences that develop analytical thinking, investigative reasoning, and confidence in forensic science applications.
