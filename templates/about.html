📘 Who We Are

Bridging Academic Knowledge with Real-World Forensics

Secrets in the Stains is a forensic science education and training platform designed to help students move beyond theoretical learning and develop practical, career-oriented exposure to forensic investigation.

Our goal is to create accessible, structured and professionally designed learning experiences that encourage analytical thinking, investigative reasoning and confidence in forensic science applications.
